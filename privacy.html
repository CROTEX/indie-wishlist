Privacy Policy

Indie Wishlist Uploader · Last updated October 27, 2026

What this app is

Indie Wishlist Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the Indie Wishlist YouTube channel (@IndieWishlistHQ) to upload, schedule and update videos on that channel. It is not offered to the public and does not collect information from anyone else.

Google data the app accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the app requests these YouTube permissions:

  • youtube.upload – to upload videos to the owner's channel.
  • youtube.readonly – to read the channel's own videos and their status (for example, to confirm an upload or its scheduled time).
  • youtube.force-ssl – to update the titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails and privacy or schedule settings of the owner's own videos.

How the data is used and stored

  • The data is used only to publish and manage videos on the owner's own channel.
  • The sign-in token is stored only on the channel owner's computer. It is not sent to any server other than Google's.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • No personal information about viewers or other YouTube users is collected or stored.

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app also uses YouTube API Services and is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the token from the owner's computer also removes the app's access.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact the channel through its YouTube About page.
